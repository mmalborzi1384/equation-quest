# Clarifications — fractional lesson

Owner explicitly approved inclusion of rational coefficients and exact fractional answers in v1 and requires independent fraction examples, exercises, step explanations and feedback. Fraction content is not optional/bonus/end-only. `ax+b=c`, `a≠0` with rational a,b,c defines v1; variable in a denominator (e.g. 1/x) would be a different equation class and is not silently added. Curriculum/Persian copy review is required before public release, not claimed now.
