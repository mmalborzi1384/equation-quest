# Independent fractional equation lesson and practice

**Status:** Phase 1 planning baseline approved by A1/A2; **not implemented**. Additional independent feature required to preserve all eight original core topics and give fractions a first-class route.

## Story and objective
As a grade-7 learner who has practised small integers and signed/inverse operations, I can use equal-parts visuals to solve `ax+b=c` with signed rational coefficients/constants and an exact rational answer, explaining each equivalent operation and verifying by substitution. This is a separate, repeatable lesson/practice experience at `/learning/fractions`, not a few bonus problems at the end of the game.

## Required interaction
Provide distinct worked examples, multiple graded exercises, successive hints for denominator/reciprocal/sign/one-sided/rounding errors, retry/new example, LTR KaTeX and an accessible non-WebGL fraction representation. At least one example has fractional coefficient with integer result, another integer coefficient with fractional result and another fractional coefficient with fractional result. Difficulty grows from equal parts to one-step rational actions to a two-step `ax+b=c`, always checking exact equivalence; link to `/learning/inverse-operations`, `/learning/solve`, `/learning/verify` and levels 06–08.

## Acceptance evidence (future implementation)
Direct hash route and lesson navigation; guided fractional step and feedback tests; exact answer/substitution tests; signed fraction and zero-denominator handling; mobile/keyboard/reduced-motion/non-WebGL checks; Anti-slop gate. Phase 1 approves specification only; no runnable lesson exists.
