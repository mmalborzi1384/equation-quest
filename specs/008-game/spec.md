# 3D exploration and controls

**Status:** Draft / awaiting owner approval. **Branch:** arena/5b2fda5d-equation-quest.

## User story
As a seventh-grade learner, I can use this feature to understand and practice equations without creating an account.

## Requirements
Isometric player moves with keyboard/touch, interacts with labelled objects, pauses/resumes; quality can change without losing puzzle.

## Scope and constraints
R3F scene, orthographic follow camera, game-engine input state, HUD and mobile controls. Persian RTL, mathematical LTR, desktop/mobile, accessible and usable without backend. Shared navigation and no dead controls. Refer to PRD, ROUTES, EDUCATIONAL_CONTENT and constitution for cross-cutting requirements.

## Acceptance evidence required
keyboard arrows/WASD E Esc, mobile joystick/tap/pause, fallback and presets. Do not mark completed without implementation, tests and anti-slop delivery gate.
