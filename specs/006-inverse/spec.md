# Inverse operations lesson

**Status:** Draft / awaiting owner approval. Portable specification; implementation branch is assigned per Arena session.

## User story
As a seventh-grade learner, I can use this feature to understand and practice equations without creating an account.

## Requirements
Student chooses inverse operation and applies it to both sides; sees why one-sided or zero division is invalid.

## Scope and constraints
Inverse pair exercise and animated before/after, pure math operation API. Persian RTL, mathematical LTR, desktop/mobile, accessible and usable without backend. Shared navigation and no dead controls. Refer to PRD, ROUTES, EDUCATIONAL_CONTENT and constitution for cross-cutting requirements.

## Acceptance evidence required
all 4 operations, division by zero, unilateral change and reduced motion. Do not mark completed without implementation, tests and anti-slop delivery gate.

## Phase 1 approved scope (A1–A5, including strengthened A2)
Teach inverse addition/subtraction and multiplication/division first on signed integers, then fractions and reciprocals with visual explanation, nonzero divisors and exact equivalence; use the pure math checker. This is a planning contract, not implemented behavior.
