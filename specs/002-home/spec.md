# Home narrative and glass carousel

**Status:** Draft / awaiting owner approval. Portable specification; implementation branch is assigned per Arena session.

## User story
As a seventh-grade learner, I can use this feature to understand and practice equations without creating an account.

## Requirements
All ten home sections convey project, journey, world and controls; a carousel card opens its real destination.

## Scope and constraints
GSAP/ScrollTrigger only for scroll narration; web DOM/CSS/SVG glass lens with pointer/keyboard, snap/focus and fallback. Persian RTL, mathematical LTR, desktop/mobile, accessible and usable without backend. Shared navigation and no dead controls. Refer to PRD, ROUTES, EDUCATIONAL_CONTENT and constitution for cross-cutting requirements.

## Acceptance evidence required
desktop/mobile carousel links, focus controls, reduced motion, no-WebGL reading path. Do not mark completed without implementation, tests and anti-slop delivery gate.
