# Pure equation engine specification — required rational scope (not implemented)

## Domain boundary and representation
Version 1 solves **`ax+b=c`** with `a≠0`, including **signed integer and rational `a`, `b`, `c` and exact rational `x`**. Here a fraction means a constant-denominator rational number (e.g. `x/2+1/4=3/4`, `(2/3)x+1/2=5/6`), not a variable in a denominator such as `1/x` (outside the authorized linear form). The required fractional scope is part of v1, **not deferred to an optional extension**.

Proposed pure types: `Rational { numerator: bigint; denominator: bigint }` normalized to a positive nonzero denominator, gcd-reduced with zero as `0/1`; `LinearEquation { a: Rational; b: Rational; c: Rational }`, `a.numerator !== 0`. Input/display boundaries normalize permitted integer/fraction strings (including Persian digits where supported) and reject empty, NaN, infinity, zero denominator and `a=0`; do **not** use JavaScript floating-point equality or a magic tolerance to grade exact fractions. Never serialize BigInt directly to JSON/LocalStorage: serialize canonical numerator/denominator decimal strings and validate on restore. Author small denominators/values by difficulty to control BigInt growth and cognitive load.

## Generator and difficulty policy
Generator accepts a deterministic seed and difficulty constraints. Pick rational `a≠0`, `b` and target `x`, calculate exact `c=a*x+b`; constrain denominators and magnitude for each band. Bands: small integer operations → signed integer coefficients/constants → simple fractions/reciprocals → two-step rational-coefficient equations and verification. Fraction sets must include rational coefficient + integer result, integer coefficient + fractional result, and rational coefficient + fractional result. Fractions appear in an independent lesson, level 06 introduction, level 07 development and level 08 integrated assessment, plus repeatable supplementary exercises — not only at the game's end.

## Step model and mathematical semantics
Represent each side as a linear expression with rational coefficients `(coefficientOfX, constant)`. Typed actions `add|subtract|multiply|divide` with a nonzero divisor where required, and sides `both|left|right`; the checker validates resulting algebraic equivalence, the **same operation on both sides**, and the pedagogically relevant next step. One-sided operations may be shown as a teaching error but cannot silently become a valid solution. A valid guided path for `ax+b=c`: subtract `b` on both sides → simplify → divide by nonzero `a` (or multiply by its reciprocal) on both sides → report exact `x` → substitute into the **original** equation and verify both sides are exactly equal. Equivalent reduced fractions such as `2/4` and `1/2` compare as equal; formatting is not correctness.

Worked fractional path: `(2/3)x+1/2=5/6` → subtract `1/2` on both sides → `(2/3)x=1/3` → multiply both sides by `3/2` → `x=1/2` → substitute and verify `1/3+1/2=5/6`. KaTeX renders the math in LTR isolation; explanatory text remains Persian RTL. Present numerator/denominator visually as equal parts before symbolic shortcuts. The engine itself imports neither React nor Three.js.

## Feedback and hints
Categorize at least `unilateral-change`, `wrong-inverse`, `sign-error`, `division-by-zero`, `zero-denominator`, `fraction-denominator-error`, `reciprocal-error`, `premature-rounding`, `answer-only`, `arithmetic-error`, `verification-mismatch`, `correct`. Progressive hints first reference the equality/part model, then the term to undo, then a choice of next operation; do not reveal a final answer immediately. Correct steps include the mathematical reason, not merely a success badge.

## Test contract and extension
Unit tests must cover normalization, sign rules, zero denominator/coefficient rejection, exact arithmetic without float drift, nonzero divisors, seeded solvable generation across all bands, equivalent fractions, fractional coefficient with integer/fractional solution, negative fractions, correct and incorrect both-sided operations, hint progression, exact substitution and duplicate attempts. Boundary tests cover Persian input normalization and serialized rational round-trip. Extend the pure equation/step interface for any later equation class; do not hide generalized rational equations or unbounded parsing inside v1.
